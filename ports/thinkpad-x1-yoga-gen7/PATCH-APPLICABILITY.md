# Graphics patch applicability: X1 Yoga Gen 7

Assessment against repository revision `99631db20ad841bb5d5f3934e4ffa4dc09f92dee`. “Candidate” means the source or a close hardware report makes reuse plausible; it does not mean validated on the Yoga.

Target-side Linux inventory and firmware captures have been reviewed on the main PC. The USB manifest lists 51 files with no missing captures. i915 reports 96 available EUs across six subslices and an active eDP pipe on two lanes at a 270 MHz port clock; its separate all-zero enabled-SSEU snapshot is not fused topology. Static VBT decoding finds a generic 1920×1200 timing, but no AUO PnP match to prove that VBT entry is active. None of this establishes macOS behavior; raw ACPI/VBT files remain private and out of Git. See EVIDENCE-ANALYSIS.md.

## Machine-specific components to replace

- ACPI: do not reuse `SSDT-XPS-I2C.aml`, its `_INI`/`_CRS` renames, or XPS GPIO paths. The Yoga DSDT maps PC00.I2C0.TPD0 to SYNA8016 (06CB:CEB3, I2C address 0x2C) and PC00.I2C1.TPL1 to WACF2200 (056A:5308, address 0x0A). Firmware methods fill GPIO/interrupt resources; derive and validate those separately.
- USB: do not reuse `UTBDefault.kext` or the XPS `USBToolBox` map. Build from Yoga port enumeration.
- SMBIOS: do not copy the XPS MacBookPro16,2 identity or generate serials in this research scaffold.
- Audio and sensors: do not reuse the XPS SOF/SoundWire amplifier path or Dell sensor kexts for the Yoga's ALC287.
- Installation fallback: the repository's `config-install.plist` still has the XPS GPU spoof, ACPI tables and USB map. It is not a Yoga recovery config.

## Dependency and reproducibility findings

- NootedGreen is pinned by `install.sh` to commit `95f81898be65cb9f7b3d370ca78054be4cc8a62`; Lilu build headers are fetched as 1.7.2 while the EFI contains Lilu 1.7.3.
- MacKernelSDK is cloned at moving HEAD in the NootedGreen, HookCase and VoodooGPIO builders. VoodooGPIO source is pinned to `717ab2a`.
- OpenCore 1.0.8 is pinned for the `macserial` download in `make-efi.sh`; the tracked EFI binary's exact version still needs confirmation.
- No dependency pin should be invented for a Yoga build. Record the exact SDK, kext, OpenCore and Apple bundle versions used after a successful validation build.
